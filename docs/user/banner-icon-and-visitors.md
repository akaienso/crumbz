# The banner, the icon, and what visitors see

## First visit

A visitor sees the banner. It offers:

- **Accept all**: allows every category and accepts every document.
- **Reject all**: allows only what the site needs to work.
- **Customize**: lets the visitor choose each category, and accept each document, before saving.
- **×**, or pressing Escape: counts as Reject all.

Nothing optional runs and no optional cookie is set until the visitor clicks Accept, or saves choices with a category ticked.

If a visitor leaves without choosing, nothing is saved. The banner returns the next time they visit.

## After a choice

A round icon with a cookie picture appears, usually in the bottom-left corner. The visitor can:

- **Tap** it to reopen the settings and change their choices, review the documents they accepted, or switch the theme.
- **Drag** it to another spot. If the visitor has accepted Preferences, the spot is remembered for the next visit. If they have not, it goes back to the default corner on each page.
- Use the **arrow keys** after tabbing to it, to nudge it.
- Choose **Hide this icon** to hide it. Clearing the browser's cookies brings it back, along with the banner.

## Themes

The banner and dialog follow the visitor's system light or dark setting by default. The **Theme** button cycles through Auto, Light, and Dark. The choice is remembered only if the visitor has accepted Preferences.

## California visitors

The settings dialog shows **Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information**. If the browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, Crumbz keeps marketing off, even after Accept all, and shows a note.

## Reset

Clearing cookies resets everything: the banner returns, and the icon position and theme are forgotten.
