# Jurisdictions and the advisor

Crumbz applies the rules you choose to every visitor. It does not look up each visitor's location. Choose the rules that cover where your business is based and who you serve.

## Choose with the advisor

Go to **Crumbz > Jurisdiction advisor**.

1. **Where is the business?** Enter the country, the state or region (use the two-letter code for US states, such as `CA` or `NY`), the postal code, and optionally the address.
2. **Who do you serve?** Tick every group that visits the site, buys from you, or signs up. If you are not sure, tick it.
3. Save. The advisor lists the laws that likely apply, the reason for each, and the Crumbz setting it maps to.
4. **Apply suggested rules** turns on the suggested modes. You can change them later in Settings.

The advisor's suggestions come only from your answers. It makes no call to any outside service.

Location hints appear above the form. When the site runs behind Cloudflare, the country your connection reports is shown. Your browser's language is shown too, marked as a language setting, not a location. These are suggestions. Choose the country yourself.

## What each mode does

| Mode | Applies to | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **EU mode** | The EU, the EEA, and the UK | Optional cookies need opt-in consent. Nothing is pre-ticked. Accept and Reject have equal weight. Content is never blocked. |
| **California mode** | California residents | Adds the Do Not Sell or Share link. Turns marketing off when the browser sends Global Privacy Control. |

Turn on both if you serve both groups. The stricter rule applies to each category.

## Laws without a mode yet

The advisor also lists laws Crumbz has no mode for. These are notes, not settings:

- **Other US states.** Many states have comprehensive privacy laws, and several require honoring Global Privacy Control. California mode is the nearest current setting. Check which states apply.
- **Canada, Brazil, and Australia.** Their privacy laws differ. EU mode is the strictest opt-in setting Crumbz offers. Confirm what your lawyer advises.

## Keep it current

Laws change, and so do the lists of states and countries. Review your jurisdictions when you add a market, and whenever a law is in the news for your area. Changing the jurisdictions asks every visitor to choose again.

This advisor is a starting point, not legal advice.
