# Crumbz user guide

Crumbz is a free cookie consent plugin for WordPress. It blocks optional cookies and third-party scripts until a visitor chooses.

- [Setup and settings](setup-and-settings.md): install, choose your options, link your policy pages.
- [The banner, the icon, and what visitors see](banner-icon-and-visitors.md)
- [Jurisdictions and the advisor](jurisdictions-and-advisor.md): which rules apply to your site, and how to decide.
- [Policy wizard](policy-wizard.md): draft your Cookie Policy, Privacy Policy, and Terms pages.
- [FAQ](faq.md)

Crumbz is not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review your policies before you publish them.
