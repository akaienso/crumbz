# Policy wizard

The wizard drafts three pages: a Cookie Policy, a Privacy Policy, and Terms and Conditions. It asks eleven questions, one per screen.

1. Business name
2. Email address for privacy questions
3. Type of site (blog, online store, services, membership, nonprofit, or other)
4. Analytics, such as Google Analytics, Site Kit, Jetpack Stats, or Matomo
5. Ads, retargeting, or social media tracking
6. A newsletter or promotional email list
7. Contact, quote, or booking forms
8. Accounts and logins
9. Selling products or taking payments
10. Which laws to cover (EU, California)
11. Country and state or province of the business

Then choose **Create draft pages**.

## What you get

- Three draft pages in **Pages**, with the title "Cookie Policy", "Privacy Policy", or "Terms and Conditions".
- The drafts are linked in **Crumbz > Settings > Documents**.
- Nothing is published. Visitors cannot see a draft, and Crumbz does not list it as a document until you publish it.

## Before you publish

1. Open each draft and fill in every item in square brackets, such as `[retention period]`, `[payment provider name]`, or `[Link to your refund policy]`.
2. Check the sentences that depend on your tools. The Cookie Policy lists the tools Crumbz found, or a placeholder when it found none.
3. Ask a qualified lawyer to review the text.
4. Publish each page.

The drafts are a starting point. They are not legal advice.

Running the wizard again creates a new set of drafts and points Settings at them. It never changes drafts you already have. Delete any drafts you no longer need.
